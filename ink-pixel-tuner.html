Ink Pixel Tuner

Dial in the two-color pixel and dither look on live 3D objects. Every slider matches a parameter in the Unreal post process material. Drag to turn the object, scroll to zoom, and hold the middle mouse button to pan.

Reference

You can load your own .fbx model. It's centered and scaled to fit. Embedded textures are kept; textures saved as separate files are not.
